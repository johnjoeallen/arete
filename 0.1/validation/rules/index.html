    Rule Catalogue¶

    Every rule in the bundled Speculate Policy Engine policy bundle, grouped by category. A rule is only evaluated when a policy references it. See the policy engine for how rules, detectors, and policies fit together.

    There are 139 rules across 20 categories, built on 45 detectors.

    Reading the policy columns

    A number is the point deduction applied once if the rule matches; P marks a PROHIBITED disposition (any match forces the score to 0); a blank cell means that policy does not include the rule.
